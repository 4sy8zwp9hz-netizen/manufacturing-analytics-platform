Metric definitions and interpretation
Weighted yield
Total passing die divided by total tested die for wafers in the filtered cohort.
Tool comparison
Final wafer yield grouped by exposure to a tool at the selected operation; it is a screening comparison and does not control for product, time, or other confounders.
Time filter
Applied to the timestamp of the final synthetic wafer-test yield result.
